Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Japa Mala counter track my daily practice?

The Japa Mala counter logs your counts locally to your device's secure offline database. When signed in, counts are synchronized across your devices using a daily aggregation rule.

Can I use the app without an internet connection?

Yes. Mantra is built with an offline-first architecture. All sacred texts, previously viewed mantras, and downloaded audio tracks function seamlessly offline.

How can I report a typo or incorrect Sanskrit sloka?

We work with certified Vedic scholars to maintain textual purity. If you notice a typo in any Devanagari text, email our editorial desk at vedic@aarambhtech.in with the mantra title and correction.
